Music

Dvořák, Te Deum — a new edition

The public-domain edition of Dvořák's Te Deum is old and muddy, with questionable text and note values. I made a fresh edition in Dorico that matches the public-domain score page for page, so the two can sit side by side in rehearsal.

It's a rehearsal score, not an accompanist edition. An accompanist edition and a more freely laid-out one are planned.
